# Kite Prep

SAT and PERT practice that works like an app on your phone: a Bluebook-style question bank, a Desmos Lab with replays and a practice pad, animated videos for every math question, PERT test-day prep, and word cards.

## Put it on your phone

**iPhone:** open the site in **Safari**, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
**Android:** open it in **Chrome** and tap **Install app** (or menu → **Add to Home screen**).

It opens full screen like an app and keeps working offline. Your progress, scores, and test date are saved on your phone only.

## Files

- `index.html` – the whole app
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/` – app name and home-screen icon
- `sw.js` – offline cache
- `vendor/` – jQuery (MIT) and MathQuill (MPL-2.0) for the Desmos-style math keyboard; see `LICENSES.md`
